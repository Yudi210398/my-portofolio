# Portfolio Content Publication Policy

## Project status and visibility
- `candidate` (CANDIDATE): a possible project for the portfolio. Its details and public suitability are not yet verified.
- `draft` (DRAFT): content being prepared or reviewed. It is not approved for public release.
- `published` (PUBLISHED): the owner has verified the project, its claims and assets, and explicitly approved its public release.
- Render only `published` projects in production routes. Do not treat inclusion in the brief, a draft, or a repository as publication approval. Batik Management App and MIFO are currently CANDIDATE.

## Claims and assets
- Do not invent or imply years of experience, achievements, career statistics, certifications, employers, clients, feature completion, or project results.
- Publish claims only when the owner has checked their accuracy. Numeric achievements require supporting evidence and owner approval.
- Use only real project screenshots cleared of private, customer, or production data. Label neutral development placeholders as illustrative; do not present them as actual product screens.
- Do not present an AI-generated human portrait as the owner's photograph. The approved hero direction is abstract/laptop/geometric; a personal photograph remains TODO.

## Contact and external destinations
- Public email, GitHub URL, LinkedIn URL, and CV PDF are TODO. Hide each corresponding action until the actual destination or file is provided and approved. Do not use dummy addresses or URLs.
- Check each external URL and downloadable asset with the owner before publication.

## Release gate
Before a project or claim becomes public, the owner verifies its facts, checks screenshots and other assets for sensitive data, and explicitly approves the specific content and destination. Unresolved TODO items remain unresolved and do not imply approval.
